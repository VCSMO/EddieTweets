How to format your script

Splitting into slides. Number each tweet with 1. 2. 3. at the start of a line. Use a blank line inside a tweet to mark a paragraph break (which becomes a visual gap on the slide).

1. Most agency owners are chasing clients.

The smart ones are building systems.

One scales. The other burns you out.

2. I don't think tasks. I think habits, outcomes, and identity.

That's what core values are for.

3. ...

Also accepted: 1) markers, or --- on its own line as a separator.

Word styling. Inside any tweet you can emphasize text with:

  • **bold** → bold
  • *italic* → italic
  • ***both*** → bold italic

Asterisks only activate around non-space content, so things like 5 * 6 = 30 render normally.
